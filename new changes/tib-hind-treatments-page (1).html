Heart treatment in India

Cardiac care is where TIB HIND is strongest, because it is Dr. Varughese's own field. She is a board-certified interventional cardiologist, and she reads every cardiac file that reaches us before anyone else sees it.

Procedures we arrange

  • Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG)
  • Angioplasty and stent placement
  • Heart valve repair and replacement
  • TAVI — transcatheter aortic valve implantation
  • Pacemaker and ICD implantation
  • Paediatric surgery for congenital defects (ASD, VSD, tetralogy of Fallot)
  • Aortic aneurysm repair

Conditions commonly treated

  • Coronary artery disease and angina
  • Heart valve stenosis or regurgitation
  • Heart failure and cardiomyopathy
  • Arrhythmia and conduction disorders
  • Congenital heart defects in children
  • Aortic aneurysm and dissection
What moves the cost

The number of grafts or stents, the type of valve used — mechanical valves last longer, tissue valves avoid lifelong blood thinners — and how many days you spend in intensive care.

Cancer treatment in India

Cancer care is rarely one procedure. It is a sequence — diagnosis, staging, surgery, and often months of systemic treatment. We plan the whole sequence before you fly, so you are not making decisions about the next step from a hotel room.

A second opinion is often the cheapest thing you can buy. Send the reports before you commit to a plan anywhere — including here.

Procedures we arrange

  • Surgical oncology, including minimally invasive and robotic resection
  • Chemotherapy
  • Radiotherapy — IMRT, IGRT and stereotactic techniques
  • Immunotherapy and targeted therapy
  • Bone marrow transplant for blood cancers
  • Second opinion on a diagnosis made elsewhere

Conditions commonly treated

  • Breast, cervical and ovarian cancer
  • Lung and head and neck cancer
  • Colorectal, gastric and liver cancer
  • Prostate and bladder cancer
  • Leukaemia, lymphoma and myeloma
  • Paediatric cancers
What moves the cost

Stage at diagnosis, the number of chemotherapy cycles, whether the protocol uses biologic or targeted drugs — these can exceed the cost of surgery — and which radiotherapy technique is indicated.

Joint replacement in India

Joint replacement is the most predictable work we arrange. The procedures are well standardised, recovery is measurable, and the cost difference against Europe or the Gulf is at its widest here.

Doing both knees in one admission usually costs less than two separate trips, but it is not right for every patient. That is a clinical decision, not a commercial one.

Procedures we arrange

  • Total knee replacement, single or both knees
  • Total hip replacement
  • Robotic-assisted joint replacement
  • Revision surgery where a previous implant has failed
  • Shoulder replacement
  • ACL reconstruction and arthroscopy

Conditions commonly treated

  • Advanced osteoarthritis of the knee or hip
  • Avascular necrosis of the femoral head
  • Rheumatoid and inflammatory arthritis
  • Loosened, infected or failed previous implants
  • Post-traumatic joint damage
What moves the cost

Implant choice is the biggest single factor — material and manufacturer can change the total substantially. Then whether both joints are done in one admission, whether robotic assistance is used, and how complex a revision is.

Organ transplant
Full specialty page → Published cost range →

Organ transplant in India

Transplant is the most tightly regulated treatment we arrange, and the one where clear information matters most. Read the legal position before you plan anything else.

Indian law permits transplant for foreign nationals only where the donor is a near relative, with the relationship certified by your embassy and every case approved by a hospital Authorisation Committee. We cannot find, source, match or arrange a donor — nobody legally can. If any company tells you otherwise, walk away.

Procedures we arrange

  • Living-donor liver transplant
  • Kidney transplant from a living related donor
  • Paediatric liver and kidney transplant
  • Pre-transplant assessment and donor workup
  • Post-transplant follow-up arranged from your home country

Conditions commonly treated

  • Cirrhosis from hepatitis B or C, alcohol or fatty liver disease
  • Acute liver failure
  • End-stage renal disease
  • Biliary atresia and metabolic liver disease in children
What moves the cost

Donor workup, length of intensive care stay, immunosuppressive drug protocol, and whether complications such as rejection or infection occur.

Bone marrow transplant in India

Thalassemia and sickle cell disease are common across Iraq, Sudan, Yemen and Bangladesh, and transplant is the only treatment that offers a cure. Most of the families who contact us about this are asking on behalf of a child.

If a sibling may be a donor, HLA typing for the whole family is the first step and can often be done in your own country. Ask us before you travel.

Procedures we arrange

  • Matched sibling donor transplant
  • Haploidentical (half-matched parent) transplant
  • Matched unrelated donor transplant
  • Autologous transplant for myeloma and lymphoma
  • HLA typing and donor matching for family members

Conditions commonly treated

  • Thalassemia major
  • Sickle cell disease
  • Aplastic anaemia
  • Acute and chronic leukaemia
  • Lymphoma and multiple myeloma
  • Primary immunodeficiency in children
What moves the cost

Donor type — a matched sibling is the least complex and least expensive route — the conditioning regimen used, days in protective isolation, and whether graft-versus-host disease develops.

IVF treatment in India

Fertility treatment is emotionally punishing and frequently oversold. We will tell you what the realistic prospect is for your age and diagnosis before you spend anything, and that conversation is free.

Surrogacy is not legally available to foreign nationals in India under the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act 2021, and we cannot arrange it. Sex selection is a criminal offence under the PCPNDT Act and we will not discuss it in any form.

Procedures we arrange

  • IVF with fresh or frozen embryo transfer
  • ICSI for male factor infertility
  • IUI
  • Blastocyst culture and vitrification
  • Egg, sperm and embryo freezing
  • Surgical sperm retrieval (TESA, PESA, micro-TESE)

Conditions commonly treated

  • Blocked or damaged fallopian tubes
  • Male factor infertility and low sperm count
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome
  • Endometriosis
  • Low ovarian reserve and low AMH
  • Recurrent miscarriage and unexplained infertility
What moves the cost

How many cycles are needed, the stimulation protocol and drug doses, whether ICSI is required, and whether embryos are frozen for later transfer.

Why India

What makes treatment in India genuinely worth considering

Seven reasons patients travel here, and the specific thing that makes each one real rather than a slogan. The strength shown is our own assessment, not a rating anyone awarded us.

01

Cost

Substantial

For complex surgery the difference is transformative. A liver transplant, a bone marrow transplant for thalassemia, or a cardiac operation costs a fraction of the European or Gulf price. We tell you precisely where your case sits, flights and accommodation included, so the figure you plan around is the real one.

02

Surgical expertise

Exceptional

Indian surgeons operate at volumes rarely matched anywhere, because of the population they serve. A Delhi cardiac surgeon may perform more valve operations in a year than a European colleague does in five. We select on that number — annual volume in your procedure, the measure most closely linked to outcome.

03

Speed of access

Decisive

Where you might wait months for a scan, then an opinion, then a date, the whole sequence can be completed in weeks. For some conditions that is clinical, not convenient — a child's scoliosis progressing through a growth spurt, or cervical myelopathy where lost function does not return.

04

Accreditation

A solid foundation

JCI and NABH accreditation confirms international standards for safety, infection control and patient process, and we work only with accredited units. We then go one step further, matching you to the unit with the highest volume in your specific procedure.

05

Technology

Excellent, used well

Intraoperative neuromonitoring, neuronavigation, conformal radiotherapy, endoscopic ultrasound and robotic platforms are all available and genuinely change outcomes. We verify the specific equipment your case needs is on site rather than assuming a well-known name implies it.

06

Breadth of treatment

Comprehensive

Twenty-six specialties, from routine procedures to transplant and complex reconstruction, within one region. Working in Delhi NCR alone is deliberate — we have visited these units, know their teams, and can move you between specialties without starting again.

07

International patient care

Well established

Indian hospitals have run international services for decades, with interpreters, visa letters, dietary provision and coordinators as standard. We add a clinician-led layer: a doctor reads your reports before any commercial conversation, and the same team follows you home.

These advantages are strongest for complex, planned treatment and weakest for straightforward day surgery, where the journey can outweigh the saving. Send your reports and we will tell you exactly which apply to your case — and where the benefit is genuinely large, we will say so.
