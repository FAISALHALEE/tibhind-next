04 · Care pathway

The doctor’s role in cardiac care

Cardiac care can involve clinical cardiologists, imaging specialists, interventional cardiologists, electrophysiologists, anaesthesia, intensive care and cardiac or vascular surgeons.

✓ Review the diagnosis, imaging and suitability for the proposed procedure
✓ Explain non-procedural and procedural alternatives
✓ Coordinate anaesthesia, imaging, intensive-care or surgical backup where needed
✓ Discuss material risks, devices, recovery and warning signs
✓ Define medicines and follow-up responsibilities

07 · International patients

Consultation and travel planning

Before travel, request the clinical opinion available from the records, tests likely to be repeated, proposed hospital, expected admission pathway, indicative estimate and follow-up plan. Final decisions are made after the responsible clinical team completes its assessment.

Emergency warning: sudden or persistent chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, new weakness or confusion may require urgent local care. Do not delay emergency assessment to arrange international travel.
